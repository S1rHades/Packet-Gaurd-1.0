<p><b>Packet Guard</b> helps you open sites that your network blocks by inspecting traffic (DPI). It runs the <a href='https://github.com/hufrea/byedpi' target='_blank' rel='nofollow noopener'>ByeDPI</a> engine on your phone, finds the setting that works on your connection, and keeps it on.</p>
<p>There is no VPN server and no account. Your traffic goes straight to the sites you visit. The app does not send it to any remote server, does not encrypt it and does not hide your IP address. It also cannot help when a site is blocked by its address rather than by inspecting traffic.</p>
<p>
<b>What it does</b><br>
* Connect screen: tap the shield to turn protection on or off, with live latency and success rate<br>
* Benchmark: tests ready-made settings on the site lists you pick, scores each out of 100 for sites opened, success and speed, and recommends one<br>
* Choose the fastest or most reliable setting instead, and see what you gain and give up<br>
* Command line generator: builds and tests new command lines for your own connection, and learns from the results<br>
* Saved strategies, with the last test result next to each<br>
* Every command line explained in plain words<br>
* VPN mode for the whole phone or only for apps you choose (split tunnelling), or local proxy mode<br>
* Quick Settings tile and auto-start when the phone boots or the app opens<br>
* In-app updates from GitHub releases (can be switched off)<br>
* Export and import of settings<br>
* English, Russian, Turkish, Kazakh and Vietnamese
</p>
<p>The Benchmark and the live check contact the sites in the lists you switch on. The app contacts GitHub only to check for updates. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.</p>
